Services · CMS-0057-F

Know your FHIR APIs pass before January 1, 2027.

We run the ONC Inferno test kits against your CMS-0057-F APIs, inside your own environment. You get results by implementation guide, a list of gaps with fixes, and a re-test.

Schedule a scoping call All services

Why inside your environment

The public Inferno instance is for demonstration. It is not for PHI. We deploy the open-source test kits in your cloud and test real endpoints with real data. Nothing leaves your boundary.

What gets tested

Your APIInferno test kits
Patient AccessCARIN Blue Button, US Core, SMART App Launch
Provider Access and Payer-to-PayerDa Vinci PDex, Bulk Data

FormularyDa Vinci US Drug Formulary
Prior AuthorizationDa Vinci CRD, DTR, and PAS

What you get

  1. A self-hosted Inferno environment in your cloud, left in place for regression testing.
  2. Results for each test kit, with failures mapped to the implementation guide requirement.
  3. A remediation plan sorted by severity, ready for your vendor or internal team.
  4. A re-test after fixes and a short readiness summary for compliance and leadership.

Typical timeline: two to four weeks, depending on how many APIs and vendors are involved.

What this is not

This is conformance validation and readiness evidence. It is not ONC certification. ONC certifies health IT developers, not payers. We do not issue badges.
