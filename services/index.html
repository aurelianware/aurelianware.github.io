What we do

ServiceWhat the plan gets
CMS-0057-F API validation The ONC Inferno test kits run against your Patient Access, Provider Access, Payer-to-Payer, Provider Directory, and Prior Authorization APIs, inside your environment. Results, gaps, fixes, re-test.
CMS-0057-F readiness assessment A gap analysis across the required APIs and prior authorization metrics reporting, with a dated plan to close each gap.
Clearinghouse and portal integration Availity to QNXT integration: 837, 835, 270/271, 276/277, 275, appeals, and authorizations.
Cloud migration and BPaaS exit A plan-owned Azure, AWS, or GCP environment, a migration runbook, and a cutover plan off vendor-hosted operations.
Core admin advisory QNXT and Facets configuration, claims, appeals, and adjustment workflows. Vendor and SOW review before you sign.
Fractional architect A senior payer architect on your team part-time for roadmap, vendor oversight, and design review.

Who does the work

Aurelianware is led by Mark Phillips. He has worked on payer core systems since 2001: QNXT Solution Owner at Cognizant, Director of Engineering at Invidasys, and lead architect on QNXT implementations at TriZetto. He is a co-inventor of the MyQNXT patent.

Aurelianware also builds Cloud Health Office. Services do not require buying it. Every engagement is scoped and quoted individually.
