Privacy

This is a company marketing site.

It does not create accounts, store form submissions, or process PHI. Product sites publish their own notices.

What we collect

The contact page opens your own mail client. The message stays in your mail until you send it. Nothing from that form is stored on this site.

Hosting and the browser itself will see ordinary request logs (IP address, user agent, pages requested). We do not sell that data. We do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Fonts

This site loads Instrument Serif and IBM Plex Sans from Google Fonts. That is a third-party request. If you block it, the pages still work with system serif and sans-serif fallbacks.
