Company

A pre-seed software company with one product.

Aurelianware, Inc. builds and licenses Cloud Health Office, and owns its intellectual property. This page is for investors, counsel, and diligence.

Who we are

We are a product company. We build health plan core administration software, publish the source a security team can read, and license it to health plans by subscription.

We are not a consultancy, an agency, a staff-augmentation firm, a clearinghouse, or a reseller of someone else’s core admin system.

Business model

LineWhat the plan pays for
Subscription license The core revenue. An annual Cloud Health Office license priced per member per month and sized to the plan. Multi-year terms. Expansion is an amendment, not a new contract.
Layers Foothold: CMS-0057-F APIs in the plan’s cloud, with the existing core as the system of record. Domain cutover: one operational domain moves off the core. Core: Cloud Health Office becomes the claims ledger. Each layer includes the one before it.
Implementation and onboarding A supporting service, scoped with the license: standing up the plan’s file drop, APIs, and tenant. Not sold as staff time on its own.

Indicative pricing is shared during discovery. See cloudhealthoffice.com/pricing.

Licensing

Cloud Health Office is licensed under BSL 1.1. The source is public so anyone can read and evaluate it. Production use requires a commercial license from Aurelianware, Inc.

Team and stage

Mark Phillips, founder and CTO. 25 years in payer core systems across QCSI, TriZetto, Cognizant, Molina Healthcare, and Invidasys. He architected and built Cloud Health Office.

Stage. Pre-seed. The product is built and tested on synthetic data. No plan runs it in production yet. First target market: Texas Medicaid managed care plans.

What we do not claim

We do not present cloud-provider certifications as Aurelianware certifications. We do not currently hold SOC 2 Type II or HITRUST. Passing ONC Inferno kits is not CMS or ONC certification. We do not invent customer logos, throughput numbers, or “trusted by” lines. Evidence is in the repository and on cloudhealthoffice.com/evidence.
