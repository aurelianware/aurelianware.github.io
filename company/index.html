Company

The legal entity behind the products.

Aurelianware, Inc. owns the intellectual property. Cloud Health Office and Cloud Dental Office are the products. This page is for counsel, diligence, and anyone who followed a GitHub org here.

Who we are

We are a software company. We write healthcare infrastructure, publish the source that a security team can actually read, and deploy into the customer’s cloud when the product requires it.

We are not a staff-augmentation firm, a clearinghouse, or a reseller of someone else’s core admin system.

What this site is for

Company identity, a product index, and the contacts that belong on a parent: hello@aurelianware.com and hipaa@aurelianware.com. Product evaluation, pricing, pilots, and architecture live on the product sites.

What we do not claim

We do not present cloud-provider certifications as Aurelianware certifications. We do not currently hold SOC 2 Type II or HITRUST. We do not invent customer logos, throughput numbers, or “trusted by” lines on this page. Evidence is in the repositories and on the product sites that publish it.
